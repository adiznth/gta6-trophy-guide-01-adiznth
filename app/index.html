Read this first: one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as possibly missable: one meditation spot — Lucia’s balcony in Chapter 1 — disappears after that chapter, and the game has no mission replay. Meditate there the first time you can, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies, so leave them off for a trophy run. Sources: PowerPyx and GTABase, reported by Output Lag; cheats per PowerPyx, reported by Planet GTA 6.
